# SpaceSim

**A 5-dimensional, audio-only resonance flight sim — played entirely by ear with a screen reader.**

You fly a light vehicle through 5D space by resonance, not rockets. There is nothing to look
at and no steering wheel: you travel the way the Atlanteans were said to travel, by listening
and tuning sound into harmony. W, A, S and D fly and auto-tune the three spatial realms; the
two higher realms are yours to tune by ear until the beat steadies. Hold still in tune and a
regeneration bath forms around you.

Find the twelve temples, attune to each one's note to claim its key, raise your awareness, and
the Halls of Amenti at the centre of the universe will open to you.

The sky around you is worth listening to on its own. Binary stars beat slowly against their
companions; pulsars tick like metronomes you can steer toward from far away; quasars blaze at the
edge of hearing as fixed landmarks. Solar storms break over you, and a magnetar will drag your
tuning off true. Fly into a protoplanetary disk or the Stellar Nursery and the targets will not
hold still — but the worlds there are rich. And somewhere out past the middle, a dead star devours
its companion, roaring, survivable only by near-perfect resonance.

## Download and run

1. Download **`SpaceSim-vX.Y.Z-win64.zip`** from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/taljazz/SpaceSim-releases/releases/latest).
2. **Unblock it before extracting.** This clears Windows' "downloaded from the internet" flag
   so there is no SmartScreen warning: find the zip in your Downloads, open its context menu
   (Applications key, or Shift+F10), choose **Properties**, tab to the **Unblock** checkbox
   near the bottom of the General tab, check it, then press Enter on **OK**.
3. Extract the zip (right-click → Extract All), open the **SpaceSim** folder, and run
   **`SpaceSim.exe`**.

Windows 64-bit. Self-contained — no .NET installation needed. Headphones recommended.

## Accessibility

SpaceSim is built to be played entirely by ear.

- Works with **NVDA**, **JAWS**, or **SAPI** through Tolk — the screen-reader libraries ship
  with the game, so nothing extra to install.
- Spatial audio with HRTF: stars, planets, temples and beacons are heard in the direction
  they lie.
- Every state change is spoken. Nothing in the game requires sight.
- **F3** lets you choose which sound device the simulator plays through.

## Getting started

- From the main menu, choose **Begin Tutorial** for a hands-on, spoken walkthrough.
- Press **F1** at any time for the full in-game guide.
- **Learn Sounds** on the main menu is a sound dictionary — audition each cue and hear what
  it means before you meet it in flight.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W / A / S / D**, **Page Up / Page Down** | Fly |
| **Left / Right** | Turn your heading |
| **4** or **5**, then **Up / Down** | Tune the higher realms by ear |
| **J** | Full manual tuning of all five realms |
| **M** | Scanner — lock a destination and the craft steers itself |
| **L** / **T** | Anchor on a planet / ascend back to space |
| **G** | Turn the Tuaoi crystal to its next face |
| **T** (in flight) | A temple's note, or your journey compass |
| **C** | Open the Frequency Codex and re-read a recovered page |
| **R** | Spoken status report |
| **U** | Status panel, or the attunement menu while anchored |
| **P** / **Shift+P** | Drop a portal anchor / travel to one |
| **F1** | Help · **F3** Audio device · **Tab** Repeat last announcement |
